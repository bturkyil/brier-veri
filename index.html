Four things the record shows

Every number below is computed from public box scores and closing prices, with the sample size next to it. The terminal does this for every matchup, every day.

44.6%

NBA teams on the second night of a back-to-back win 44.6% of the time, against 50.7% when rested. 2,108 games.

63.5%

The home side wins 63.5% of FBS college football games, 54.4% in the NFL, 53.0% in MLB. Home advantage is a number, not a feeling.

0.20

The closing price in the NBA carries a Brier score of about 0.20 — a coin flip scores 0.25. The market is good. That is why Brier does not sell forecasts.

15

Below fifteen games, a split is not shown. The numbers exist; they just would not mean anything yet. The page says so instead of pretending.

How honest is this page

We scored our own “games like this” neighbourhood the way forecasters are scored. Lower is better; a coin flip is 0.250. The neighbourhood beats the league average everywhere except baseball, and loses to the market everywhere. That is the whole argument for showing the record instead of a prediction.
